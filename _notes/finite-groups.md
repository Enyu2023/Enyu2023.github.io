Notes on the structure of groups, beginning with group actions and the Sylow theorems.

Topics include semidirect products, the Krull–Schmidt and Jordan–Hölder theorems, solvable and nilpotent groups, central series, Hall’s criterion, and the Fitting subgroup. The PDF is titled *Notes for Abstract Algebra*.
