An introduction to the fundamental theorem of finite type invariants of knots and its relationship with chord diagrams and weight systems. The thesis develops the Kontsevich integral and configuration space integrals as universal finite type invariants.

Topics include chord and Jacobi diagrams, the Knizhnik-Zamolodchikov equation, the Kontsevich integral, and Bott-Taubes integration.
