A course essay written at Beijing Normal University in January 2023.

The essay develops Fourier analysis through finite-group representations and characters. Topics include dual groups and Pontryagin duality, Fourier inversion, Parseval–Plancherel and convolution formulas, an outline of the application to Dirichlet’s theorem on primes in arithmetic progressions, and the non-abelian Fourier transform and Wedderburn decomposition. It closes with a brief introduction to harmonic analysis on locally compact abelian groups.
