An introduction to Grothendieck’s perspective on Galois theory and its connection with the classification of covering spaces by the fundamental group.

Topics include profinite groups, the Krull topology, infinite Galois extensions, finite étale algebras, and the étale fundamental group. The notes discuss the correspondence between finite étale algebras and finite sets with a continuous action of the absolute Galois group.
